New IELTS Preparation Class

4 March 2026

Registration is now open for the new IELTS preparation program. Students will practice Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking with experienced instructors.

What the program includes

Full practice for all four sections of the IELTS test. Mock tests, feedback, and strategies to improve your score. Small class size for more individual attention.

How to register

Visit our centre or contact us to sign up. You can also take our free placement test to see your current level before joining.
