Last updated: April 2026. This notice describes how Myanmar New Era International Education Centre (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our public website, the free online English placement test, and related login-based management areas (for example staff or student portals linked from this site).

1. What we collect

Depending on what you use, we may process:

  • Public website & contact: Information you type into forms (for example name, phone, email, message) on pages such as Contact.
  • Free placement test: Answers, scores or levels generated in the test flow, and any name or contact details you enter if the test asks for them (for example to download a summary or continue a step).
  • Logged-in areas: Account or profile details needed to run classes, attendance, marks, placement records, or communication inside the centre’s management system.
  • Technical data: Browser type, approximate time of visit, and similar data collected automatically by normal website operation or by our hosting / analytics tools if enabled.
  • Device storage: Your browser may store small amounts of data (for example language preference or offline-ready content) on your device, similar to other modern websites.

2. Why we use it

We use this information only for legitimate centre purposes, for example: replying to enquiries; placing you in the right level or programme; running the placement test and keeping records; operating classes and administration; improving the website; and meeting safety or legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data to third parties for their marketing.

3. Where data is stored

Our project may store or sync content using cloud services (for example Google Firebase) configured for the centre, including site text, placement-related data, or messages, depending on how the system is set up. Data may be processed on servers outside Myanmar. We choose reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information.

4. Who can see it

Authorised Myanmar New Era staff (and, where relevant, teachers or administrators with accounts) can access data they need for teaching, placement, communication, or system maintenance. Service providers (such as hosting or email) may process data only to provide their service to us.

5. How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes above, for example while you are a prospective or enrolled learner, or as required for records, tax, or dispute resolution. When no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it where practicable.

6. Your choices

You may contact us to ask what we hold about you, to correct obvious mistakes, or to object where local law allows. For account-based areas, some details must stay accurate for safety and class operation.

7. Children

If information relates to a learner who is a minor, a parent or guardian should be involved where appropriate. If you believe we have collected a child’s data without proper authority, contact us and we will review.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when our website, placement test, or management tools change. The “Last updated” line will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised notice where the law allows.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: office@mmnea.com · Contact page · Nay Pyi Taw centre address as shown in the site footer.
